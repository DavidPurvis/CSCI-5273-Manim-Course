# Dish 17.17: Practice question 17: hidden terminals

## 17.17.question
Practice question 17. A and C cannot hear each other, but both can transmit to B. Explain why carrier sensing can fail, and how CTS can help. State why no ACK is not proof of collision, and name one cost of RTS and CTS.

## 17.17.predict
Answer each part before going on.

## 17.17.sensing
A and C each sense local silence, yet their signals interfere at B.

## 17.17.cts
B's CTS can be heard by C, even though C can't hear A. So C defers.

## 17.17.noack
A missing ACK can also mean data corruption, or a lost ACK. And RTS and CTS cost control airtime, and those control packets can collide too.

## 17.17.trap
The tempting answer says that silence at the sender means the channel is safe. What matters is interference at the receiver.

## 17.17.marks
On marks: two points for geometry and sensing, one for CTS, one for the loss interpretation, and one for the cost.
