# Dish 17.12: Practice question 12: stability

## 17.12.question
Practice question 12. Name the four convergence stages. Explain why path-vector own-AS rejection does not guarantee rapid convergence, or a stable result under arbitrary policies. Then distinguish exploration from persistent policy oscillation.

## 17.12.predict
Answer all three parts before going on.

## 17.12.stages
Detect, disseminate, compute, install.

## 17.12.rejection
Rejecting a path with your own AS number stops one kind of loop. But many loop-free candidate paths may remain, and a router can try them one after another. And nothing forces everyone's preferences to be compatible.

## 17.12.distinction
Exploration is a temporary response to incomplete news about a withdrawal or a change: routers try paths, and settle. Oscillation can persist, because the policy choices have no compatible stable assignment. With no stable answer, there's nothing to settle on.

## 17.12.trap
The tempting answer says that no loops means stable. But loop-free is a property of each path. Stability is a property of everyone's choices together.

## 17.12.marks
On marks: two points for all the stages, one for exploration, one for the policy conflict, and one for the distinction.
