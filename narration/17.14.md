# Dish 17.14: Practice question 14: commercial policy

## 17.14.question
Practice question 14. Under the lecture model, decide whether a provider-learned route may be exported to a customer, and to a peer. Classify the data paths up, peer, down, and down, up as valid or invalid. Then state the preference and topology conditions that accompany the export restriction in Gao-Rexford.

## 17.14.predict
Answer each part before going on.

## 17.14.export
A provider-learned route to a customer: yes. The customer pays you to reach the world. To a peer: no. That would have you carrying traffic between two non-customers.

## 17.14.paths
Up, peer, down is valley free, so it's valid. Down, then up is a valley: some customer would be carrying traffic between its providers. That one's invalid.

## 17.14.conditions
Gao-Rexford adds two more conditions. Prefer customer routes over peer and provider routes. And keep the customer-provider hierarchy free of cycles.

## 17.14.trap
The tempting answer says a peer hears everything. A peer hears only your own routes and your customers' routes.

## 17.14.marks
On marks: one point for each export answer, one for both path classifications, and one each for the preference and topology conditions.
