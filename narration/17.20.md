# Dish 17.20: Practice question 20: locator and handover

## 17.20.question
Practice question 20. A Mobile IP packet's home destination is H, and its care-of locator is C. Name the inner and outer destinations during forwarding through the home agent. Then state the before, during, and after S-GW and base-station data paths, in a handover with old A and target B.

## 17.20.predict
Answer both parts before going on.

## 17.20.tunnel
The inner destination stays H: that's the identity. The outer tunnel destination is C: that's the location.

## 17.20.paths
Before: S-GW, A, UE. During: S-GW, A, B, UE, through temporary forwarding. After: S-GW, B, UE, once the core endpoint changes.

## 17.20.trap
The tempting mistake rewrites the inner destination to C. It doesn't change. Only the outer destination carries the location.

## 17.20.marks
On marks: one point per inner or outer destination, and one per path phase. That's the whole test. For each point you lost, write down the actual cause: wrong scope, wrong table, wrong unit, wrong order, stale dependency, or confused role. Then try a changed example, with new numbers.
