# Dish 17.16: Practice question 16: membership and trees

## 17.16.question
Practice question 16. Explain the roles of IGMP, a receiver DR's star comma G PIM join, and a source DR's Register. Compare the dense and sparse defaults, and describe the purpose of an S comma G switch.

## 17.16.predict
Give each role before going on.

## 17.16.roles
IGMP expresses receiver interest on the LAN. The star comma G join builds receiver-side state toward the RP. And Register brings source startup traffic from the source's DR to the RP.

## 17.16.defaults
Dense mode forwards unless pruned. Sparse mode needs joins. An S comma G switch creates a source-rooted path that can avoid the RP detour.

## 17.16.trap
The tempting mistake says IGMP builds the multicast tree. It doesn't. IGMP works on the LAN, and PIM connects the routers.

## 17.16.marks
On marks: one point per role or distinction.
