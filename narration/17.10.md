# Dish 17.10: Practice question 10: Bellman-Ford

## 17.10.question
Practice question 10. X reaches neighbor V with cost 2, and neighbor W with cost 4. V advertises distance 7 to Z, and W advertises 2. Compute X's distance and next hop. If W later advertises 9 instead, recompute. And state what X trusts from its neighbors.

## 17.10.predict
Do both updates before going on.

## 17.10.first
Through V: 2 plus 7 is 9. Through W: 4 plus 2 is 6. The minimum is 6, so the next hop is W.

## 17.10.second
W says 9. Through W, 4 plus 9 is 13. V still gives 9. So X's distance becomes 9, through V.

## 17.10.trust
X trusts its neighbors' currently advertised remaining distances, and those may be stale. It doesn't see the paths behind them.

## 17.10.trap
The tempting shortcut looks only at link costs: 2 beats 4, so choose V. But the total decides, and W's total was the smaller one at first.

## 17.10.marks
On marks: two points for each correct update and next hop, and one for the information distinction.
