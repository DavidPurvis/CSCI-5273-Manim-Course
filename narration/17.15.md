# Dish 17.15: Practice question 15: audience and bandwidth

## 17.15.question
Practice question 15. Distinguish anycast and multicast. A 10 megabit per second stream has three recipients sharing the first link. Compare first-link traffic for iterated unicast and for network multicast, before branching. Then give one anycast failover limitation.

## 17.15.predict
Work out the two rates, and the distinction.

## 17.15.audience
Anycast selects one service instance for a shared address. Multicast delivers to the whole interested group.

## 17.15.rates
Iterated unicast sends three copies over the first link: 3 times 10 is 30 megabits per second. Multicast sends one copy until the branch: 10.

## 17.15.limit
One limitation: if routing switches a flow to another instance, that instance may lack the connection state. Convergence delay, or application health that routes don't reflect, would also be fair answers.

## 17.15.trap
The tempting mix-up says anycast reaches a group. It reaches one instance. It's multicast that reaches the group.

## 17.15.marks
On marks: two points for the audience distinction, two for the rates, and one for a valid limitation.
