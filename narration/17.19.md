# Dish 17.19: Practice question 19: LTE roles

## 17.19.question
Practice question 19. Match HSS, MME, S-GW, and P-GW to their roles. Draw the normal UE to Internet user-data path, and explain why the MME need not appear as a payload hop.

## 17.19.predict
Name each role, and trace the path.

## 17.19.roles
The HSS holds subscriber profiles and authentication data. The MME handles authentication, mobility, and control setup. The S-GW is the serving user-data and tunnel forwarding point. And the P-GW is the gateway to external packet networks.

## 17.19.path
User data goes UE, eNodeB, S-GW, P-GW, Internet. The MME coordinates that path, but control messages aren't payload, so it isn't a hop.

## 17.19.trap
The tempting mistake sends user data through the MME, because it appears in the diagram's middle. The MME controls the path. The gateways carry the data.

## 17.19.marks
On marks: one point per component, plus one for the path and control distinction.
